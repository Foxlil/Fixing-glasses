If the temple of your glasses breaks, you can use this model to print a replacement! 

# Print settings
- <b> Strength </b>

Wall loops: 5
<br>
Top shell layers: 5
<br>
Bottom shell layers: 5
<br>
Infill density: 25%
<br>

- <b> Support </b>

Enable support: ✔
<br>
Type: Tree
<br>
Threshold angle: 30
<br>
<br>
<i>The rest of the settings are already automatically set by the slicer.</i>
# Bill of materials
- Glass watch screw 2.5 X 1.4 X 6.0 mm
- Sand paper
- Super glue

# Assembly
1. You should see a rectangle-shaped cavity in the temple part. 
<img width="1522" height="586" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/04d63780-0fed-4e1f-91a9-5a73ae46af57" />
You should see it towards the right of the image.
Apply the super glue into the cavity. 
2. Place the hinge part into the cavity, ensuring that it is facing the right directions in accordance to the photo below:
<img width="1631" height="545" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5d663f49-4d62-449b-9ded-44abf170a95e" />
3. Screw the hinge connected to the 3d-printed temple to the hinge of the glasses.
<img width="1192" height="900" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/50a1d2cd-9d49-4737-9255-d71f30dc322c" />
4. Sand any part of the 3d print you deem necessary for a comfortable fit.
